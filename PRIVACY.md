# WaveCam — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

WaveCam is a camera app. This policy explains what it does with your information.

## The short version
**WaveCam does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you film ever leaves your phone unless you choose to share it yourself.

## Permissions the app uses
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | To show the viewfinder and capture photos and video. |
| **Microphone** | To record sound with your videos. You can switch audio off in Settings. |
| **Vibration** | For haptic clicks on the controls. |

Photos and videos are saved only to your phone's own gallery (Pictures/WaveCam and Movies/WaveCam). The app never uploads them anywhere.

## Data on your device
The app remembers your camera settings and a small list of thumbnails of recent captures, stored privately on your phone so they are there next time you open it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Your saved photos and videos stay in your gallery.

## Sharing
If you tap **Share**, Android's normal share menu opens and you choose where the file goes. WaveCam doesn't see or keep anything about that.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new date.
